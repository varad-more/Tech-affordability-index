Method and caveats

  • Rent is the Zillow Observed Rent Index — smoothed asking rents, metro level, all home types. Zillow publishes no bedroom-level metro series, so this is not a one-bedroom median and is not labelled as one.
  • Metro, not neighbourhood. "San Francisco" covers the whole metropolitan area, so it reads lower than a downtown listing.
  • Tax is computed for : federal brackets, FICA with the Social Security wage cap and the additional Medicare surtax, each state's own brackets and standard deduction, state payroll levies (CA SDI, WA PFML and WA Cares), and city income tax in New York City and Philadelphia.
  • Assumes a single filer taking the standard deduction, with no dependents, no 401(k) deferral and no itemised deductions. Your real number will differ.
  • Equity is discounted, not counted at face value. Unvested stock is not cash; private company shares default to a 0% haircut because you cannot pay rent with them.
  • The Washington, DC metro spans three tax jurisdictions and is modelled as Northern Virginia.
  • Sources: Zillow Research, Tax Foundation state brackets, federal brackets.
