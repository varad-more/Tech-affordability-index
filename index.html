The short version of the caveats

  • Two rent measures, never mixed. Census ACS covers every county but is a five-year average and includes utilities. Zillow ZORI is current and monthly but excludes utilities and covers about 1,360 counties. Switch between them above; the site never converts one into the other, because a national multiplier was tested and proved far too unreliable to publish.
  • Everything except rent is MIT's county-level budget for one adult with no children — a subsistence standard, not a comfortable one.
  • Local income tax is modelled for two cities only, New York City and Philadelphia. Counties elsewhere that levy one carry an on-screen warning.
  • Compensation is not location-adjusted, and the presets are national medians — edit the salary field to your actual offer.
  • Single filer, standard deduction, county-level geography. Your real number will differ.
