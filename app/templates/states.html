What this page will not tell you

  • The median is over counties, not people. No population figure ships with this project, so a state's median county is its 50th-most-expensive county, not the county half its residents live below. In states with many small rural counties and one large city, that pulls the middle down: California's median county is nothing like California's median resident.
  • No map on this site shades a state by what it costs. A choropleth is only honest when one value stands for the whole shaded area, and "what this state costs" never does. The state view of the map above shades the spread instead, which is a fact about the state as a whole rather than about any place inside it. For cost, switch the map to counties.
  • State tax is only part of the local picture. Local income tax is modelled for New York City and Philadelphia alone. States where counties or municipalities levy their own carry a warning above rather than a wrong number.
  • Coverage differs by rent basis. Census ACS covers essentially every county; Zillow ZORI covers about 1,360 of them, so on the Zillow basis a state's range is drawn from its larger counties only, which narrows it artificially.
