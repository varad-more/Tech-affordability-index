Affordability Index

What a place actually costs, against what you actually keep. Every US county, priced on current rent and current-year tax law rather than on a gross number in a job ad.

Where the numbers come from

  • Zillow ZORI asking rent, monthly
  • US Census ACS median gross rent, by unit size
  • MIT Living Wage every cost except rent
  • Tax Foundation state income tax
  • IRS and SSA federal brackets, FICA, wage base
  • Levels.fyi the compensation presets
  • us-atlas county boundaries
